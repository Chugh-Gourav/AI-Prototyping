### What is the PM Learning Hub?
An autonomous, editorial-grade intelligence hub designed to give product leaders a competitive edge across 4 key pillars:
1. **AI Deep Dive & Application**: Reasoning models (test-time compute, MCTS), MoE architectures, agent orchestration, and trajectory evals.
2. **Business & Economics**: Token COGS modeling, defending 80% SaaS gross margins, open vs. closed model TCO, and enterprise budget reallocation.
3. **Core Product Management**: Cultivating human discernment, product taste, and strategic judgment in the age of generative execution.
4. **Product Ideas to try**: Human-centered AI UX heuristics (Google PAIR), API-less desktop automation (Anthropic Computer Use), and production LLM design patterns.

### Key Capabilities
- **Cross-Pillar Meta-Thinking ("Connect the Dots")**: Synthesizes how technical mechanics, unit economics, and practical product strategy interconnect for every piece.
- **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Curator Mode**: Pre-staged candidate queue with **Fetch Content and Review** (autonomous Gemini discovery & URL verification), grammar polishing, and 1-click live database publishing.
- **Closed-Loop Feedback**: Instant micro-swaps, upvoting signals, and editorial critique memory recorded in SQLite.
- **100% Verified Deep Links**: Automated test harness enforces HTTP 200 responses and deep links across all catalog entries.
